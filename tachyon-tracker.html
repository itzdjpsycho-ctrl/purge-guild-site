About This Data
Tachyon Traces is the puzzle-collection layer — find the marked spots, collect the piece, cash in for Agris Gold Coin. Tachyon Legacy is a parallel combat layer with its own 100 markers and its own reward track. Coordinates below are raw in-game world X/Z — use the Copy button to grab them for the in-game World Map search or a third-party tool. Clicking a pin on the map (or the checkbox in the table) cycles it through not started → Active → Completed; that progress is saved only in your own browser (not shared with the guild) — same as the Squad Roles board on War Scores.
