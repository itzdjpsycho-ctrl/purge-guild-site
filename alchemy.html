How This Works
Click any Harmony Draught, Draught, or Elixir to expand its recipe inline — ingredients that are themselves craftable expand the same way, so you can drill from Harmony Draught all the way down to raw gathered reagents in one view. Quantities shown are per craft. Each of the 5 Draughts can substitute any of its 4 base Elixirs for the next-grade version at a 1:3 ratio (e.g. 10× a higher-grade elixir instead of 30× the base one) — the recipe shown here is the all-base-grade version. The 4 regional Harmony Draughts (Human / Demihuman / Kamasylvia / Edania) are separate items from the plain Harmony Draught and are [Party] recipes — they need multiple players' Alchemy tools contributing at once, not a solo craft. Spellbound Catalyst is bought from the Old Moon Manager NPC vendor, not crafted. Click the 🔗 on any row to open that item on bdocodex.com.

Calculator: every item in the Harmony Draughts, Draughts, and Base Elixirs lists has a qty box — type how many you want (e.g. 100) and press Enter. It cascades the full amount down through every tier automatically: each Draught and the plain Harmony Draught craft in batches of 10 per synthesis (everything else is 1-for-1), so the numbers are rounded up to whole crafts the same way the game does, and a results card above the list totals every intermediate craft count and every raw reagent needed end to end. Click ✕ on the box or the card to clear a calculation.
