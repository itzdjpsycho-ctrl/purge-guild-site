How To Read This
Each Draught block is one crafting family (Fury / Adaptation / Potential / Corruption / Berserk). Rows are shown exactly as laid out in the source spreadsheet — items and their quantities grouped together, left to right, with the row number from that sheet kept on the left for cross-reference. Colored chips carry the same meaning the original color-coding did (see the legend above) — green marks a Harmony Draught output, the four bright region badges mark which regional Harmony Draught a line feeds, and the dashed chips are section labels (Mushrooms, Misc Node, etc.) rather than items. Links go to bdolytics/bdocodex for the linked item.
